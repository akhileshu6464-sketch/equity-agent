1. TECHNOLOGY & SAAS:
   - Net Revenue Retention (NRR): [Starting ARR + Expansions - Contractions - Churn] / Starting ARR * 100 (Benchmark: >110-120%).
   - Gross Revenue Retention (GRR): [Starting ARR - Contractions - Churn] / Starting ARR * 100 (Benchmark: >85-90%).
   - LTV to CAC Ratio: LTV / CAC (Benchmark: >= 3x).
   - CAC Payback Period: CAC / (Monthly ARR per user * Gross Margin %) (Benchmark: <12-18 months).
   - SaaS Magic Number: (Quarter N ARR - Quarter N-1 ARR) * 4 / (Quarter N-1 S&M Expense) (Benchmark: >0.75x).
   - Rule of 40: YoY Revenue Growth % + FCF Margin % (Benchmark: >= 40%).
   - Burn Multiple: Net Burn (Cash Spent) / Net New ARR Generated (Benchmark: <1.5x).

2. BANKING, NBFCs & FINANCIAL SERVICES (BFSI):
   - Net Interest Margin (NIM): (Interest Income - Interest Expense) / Average Earning Assets (Benchmark: 3%-4%+).
   - CASA Ratio: (Current Account + Savings Account Deposits) / Total Deposits (Benchmark: >40%).
   - Asset Quality: Gross NPA % and Net NPA % (Benchmark: GNPA <3%, NNPA <1%).
   - Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR): Total Provisions / Gross NPAs (Benchmark: >70%-80%).
   - Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR / Tier 1): (Tier 1 + Tier 2 Capital) / Risk-Weighted Assets (Benchmark: >12%-15%).
   - For Insurance: Combined Ratio = (Incurred Losses + Expenses) / Net Earned Premiums (Benchmark: <100%).
   - For NBFCs: Cost of Funds (CoF) spread vs commercial banks; Stage-3 Assets (>90 days default); Collection Efficiency %.
   - For AMCs: Equity AUM vs Debt/Liquid AUM %; Monthly SIP inflow trend.

3. RETAIL, E-COMMERCE & CONSUMER GOODS:
   - Same-Store Sales Growth (SSSG / Like-for-Like): Sales from stores open >12 mos growth % (Benchmark: > inflation).
   - Sales per Square Foot / Meter: Net Annual Sales / Total Selling Area.
   - Gross Margin Return on Investment (GMROI): Gross Profit Dollars / Average Inventory Cost (Benchmark: >1.5x-2.0x).
   - Inventory Turnover Ratio & Days Sales of Inventory (DSI): COGS / Average Inventory.
   - For QSR: Average Order Value (AOV); Delivery vs Dine-in mix; Four-Wall Store EBITDA margin.
   - For Apparel: Full-Price Sell-Through Rate.

4. REAL ESTATE & REITS:
   - Funds From Operations (FFO) = Net Income + D&A - Gains on Property Sales.
   - Adjusted FFO (AFFO) = FFO - Recurring Maintenance CapEx - Straight-line Rent Adjustments.
   - Net Asset Value (NAV) Discount/Premium: Compare share price to (Market Value of Properties - Total Debt) per share.
   - Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate): Net Operating Income (NOI) / Property Market Value.
   - Occupancy Rate (Benchmark: >92%-95%) & Weighted Average Lease Expiry (WALE in years).

5. MANUFACTURING, INDUSTRIAL & AUTOMOTIVE:
   - Fixed Asset Turnover Ratio: Net Revenue / Net PP&E.
   - Capacity Utilization Rate: Actual Output / Maximum Output Capacity (Benchmark: 75%-85%).
   - Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC): DIO + DSO - DPO.
   - For Auto: Content Per Vehicle (CPV); ICE vs EV/Hybrid Powertrain Agnostic %; Dealer Inventory Days.

6. HEALTHCARE, PHARMA & LIFESCIENCES:
   - CDMO/CMO: Bioreactor & cleanroom capacity utilization %; Batch success rate & OTIF %; Phase I/II vs Commercial Phase III mix.
   - Diagnostics: Test Volume vs Realization per Test (ARPT); B2C vs B2B mix; Home collection contribution %.
   - API / Bulk Drugs: Backward integration % for Key Starting Materials (KSMs); High-margin complex APIs vs commoditized %.
   - R&D Productivity: R&D as % of Sales vs Phase II/III clinical pipeline depth; Patent cliff exposure in next 3-5 years.

7. OIL, GAS, MINING & ENERGY:
   - Reserve Replacement Ratio (RRR): Proved Reserves Added / Annual Production (Benchmark: >100%).
   - Finding & Development (F&D) Costs per BOE: Exploration & Development CapEx / BOE added.
   - All-In Sustaining Costs (AISC): Total operating + sustaining capex + reclamation / Units produced.
   - For Solar/Wind: Plant Load Factor (PLF / CUF %); Off-taker DISCOM creditworthiness rating.

8. INFRASTRUCTURE, EPC & DEFENCE:
   - Order Book-to-Revenue Ratio: Order Book / Annual Revenue (Benchmark: 3x-5x).
   - Contract Mix: Fixed-Price contracts vs Price-Escalation clauses.
   - Milestone Retainages: % of working capital trapped in unbilled revenue/retention money.
   - For Logistics: Tonnage growth; Empty Miles % on return trips; Warehousing storage yield per sq ft.
